# **App Name**: Soraki Product Links

## Core Features:

- Product Listing: Display a list of Soraki digital products, each in its own visually distinct card.
- Product Card: Each product is displayed on its own card, containing the product name, a one-line description, the price, and a link to the Cakto product page.
- Call to Action Button: Each product card contains a clear 'Acessar produto' call to action button with a link to the product on Cakto.
- Header Section: Display a welcome message at the top of the page with the title 'Soraki - Estudo Leve' and a friendly subheading: 'Escolha o que combina com sua jornada de estudos'.
- Footer Section: Implement a minimalist footer with the Soraki mascot and the tagline 'Um passo de cada vez'.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Soft beige (#F5F5DC) to give a warm and welcoming feeling. This will serve as a neutral backdrop that embodies simplicity and calm.
- Background color: Very light beige (#F8F8FF) - slightly lighter than the primary, offering a subtle contrast that is easy on the eyes.
- Accent color: Pale cyan (#E0FFFF) for highlights and CTAs to provide a gentle visual cue without overwhelming the user.
- Body and headline font: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for a friendly and simple aesthetic, used in both headlines and body.
- Apply a minimalist Japanese layout, ensuring the design is clean, cute, lightweight, and well-spaced, allowing for visual clarity and easy navigation.
- Use subtle and delicate icons or mini-illustrations from Soraki as optional details. These elements should be minimalistic, contributing to the cute and inviting ambiance.
- Subtle transitions or hover effects on product cards to enhance interactivity without being intrusive.
